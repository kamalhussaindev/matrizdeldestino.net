## General meaning

Arcana 10, Wheel of Fortune, is a reminder that nothing stays the same forever. It speaks of destiny's constant motion, of cycles that open and close without asking permission, and of the way every ending sooner or later brings a different beginning. Those who carry this energy in their Matriz del Destino tend to go through more turns of direction than average: moves, career changes, bonds that appear and disappear, opportunities that arrive without warning.

This energy teaches that resisting change tends to hurt more than moving with it. The life of those who carry this arcana rarely follows a straight line; instead it advances in a spiral, with stages that repeat with variations until the lesson they carry is learned. It's neither coincidence nor bad luck: it's destiny's own rhythm asking for flexibility.

Working consciously with this energy means letting go of the expectation of permanent stability and, instead, trusting that every turn of the wheel — even the one that looks like a step backward — is part of a wider movement toward something better.

## Positive and negative expressions

When this energy is lived with maturity, it becomes a remarkable ability to adapt to change and seize opportunities right as they appear, even the ones that arrive without notice. There's a genuine skill for flowing with what comes instead of resisting it. On its more difficult side, though, the feeling of being at the mercy of circumstance can take hold, of having no control over one's own life, which generates anxiety in the face of uncertainty and a tendency to feel like a victim of fate rather than its protagonist.

## In love

In relationships, this arcana tends to bring bonds marked by unexpected turns — reunions, sudden breakups, changes in status — that leave important lessons even when they aren't always comfortable. Accepting that love, too, has its own cycles helps in living these relationships without clinging to a fixed idea of how they should be.

## At work

Professionally, this energy favours those who know how to read the moment and move with it: changes of field, sudden opportunities, or projects that shift direction without warning. Those who carry this arcana tend to thrive more by reinventing themselves than by clinging to a single fixed path.

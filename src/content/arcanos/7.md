## General meaning

Arcana 7, The Chariot, speaks of the drive we need to move forward when the path calls for decisiveness and not just intention. In the Matriz del Destino, those who have this energy in their birth chart often feel a constant pull to move ahead, to avoid staying stuck waiting for conditions to be perfect. It's the archetype of will set into motion: two forces pulling in opposite directions that only move forward together when someone takes the reins firmly.

This energy isn't about brute force, but about direction. The Chariot moves forward because someone decides where it's going, even when the inner terrain is full of tension or conflicting desires. Those who carry this arcana tend to live a life marked by concrete goals and moments when they must choose a direction and hold it no matter what. Real progress happens when what is wanted becomes aligned with what is done, without letting inner doubts stall the movement.

## Positive and negative expressions

In its most luminous expression, Arcana 7 translates into discipline, determination, and an admirable ability to overcome obstacles without losing direction. It's the person who, faced with adversity, finds a way to keep going and carries those around them along thanks to their conviction. In its more challenging version, this same energy can become impulsiveness or an exhausting inner tension between what one wants to achieve and the forces pulling the other way, generating friction and clashes with the surrounding environment.

## In love

In relationships, those who have this arcana need to feel that they're moving toward something together with their partner, not just standing still beside them. They tend to live the relationship with intense passion and need to preserve their own direction, which can create friction if they don't negotiate well between the shared path and the individual one.

## At work

Professionally, Arcana 7 brings willpower to lead progress and push through obstacles that would stop others. This energy is commonly found in people who drive difficult projects forward, who don't give up at the first no, and who inspire their teams to move toward a common goal, as long as they manage to channel their determination without imposing it.

## General meaning
Arcana 16, The Tower, represents in the Matriz del Destino those moments when a structure that seemed solid collapses all at once, without warning. It's not a subtle energy: it tends to show up as a crisis, a rupture, or a revelation that completely changes the way a situation is seen. Those who carry this arcana in their matrix tend to go through, at different stages of their life, sudden breakdowns that at first feel devastating.

However, what The Tower brings down was never as stable as it seemed: they were foundations built on false ideas, relationships based on denial, or projects sustained through effort but without real grounding. The collapse, seen with perspective, ends up being the necessary step toward building something true.

## Positive and negative expressions
In its most conscious expression, this energy grants an uncommon courage to rebuild on solid foundations after a crisis, without repeating the same mistakes. People who integrate it well tend to be grateful, over time, for those breakdowns that felt like catastrophes in the moment.

When resisted, though, The Tower can generate a constant sense of instability, as if the ground could disappear at any moment. Refusing to let go of structures that no longer work only postpones the collapse and makes it harsher when it finally arrives.

## In love
In relationships, this arcana tends to bring crises that surface what hadn't been resolved: silences, distrust, or underlying disagreements that can no longer be avoided. Although painful, these breakups tend to clear the way toward more honest bonds.

## At work
Professionally, The Tower can show up as unexpected layoffs, business closures, or abrupt changes of direction that, although uncomfortable in the moment, end up opening the way to a far more solid professional foundation, one aligned with who the person truly is.

The "wellbeing line" is a concept that appears in some variants and schools of the Matriz del Destino to describe, as a group, the positions related to a person's energetic health and overall balance: body, emotions, and mind seen as a single interconnected system, rather than three separate areas.

## What it traditionally represents

In the sources that describe this concept, the wellbeing line isn't a single, isolated position, but a combined reading of several positions that are already part of the system: mainly your chakras and your Point E. The underlying idea is that overall wellbeing doesn't depend on a single number, but on how your physical energy, emotional energy, and baseline mental state interact over time.

If you notice that several of your chakras share the same arcana or a similar energy, that usually points to a priority area of attention for your overall wellbeing.

## What to do with this reading

If, when reviewing your chakras, you find the same arcana repeating in more than one position, it's worth paying special attention: it usually indicates an energy running through several layers of your wellbeing — physical, emotional, mental — at the same time, for better or worse. For example, if the same arcana of transformation (Death) appears in both your root chakra and your heart chakra, it's likely that processes of change affect you both in your physical stability and in your emotional bonds, and that consciously working with that energy in one area also benefits the other.
